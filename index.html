About Me

I'm Victor Mauti Cosmas,a Computer Scientist graduate from Machakos University with a Second Upper Honours degree. Over the past few years I've grown from learning the basics of the web into someone who ships real, deployed applications from full-stack MERN systems to blockchain canisters and Telegram trading bots.

I'm drawn to projects that solve practical problems. GovTrack Kenya, my flagship project, was built because lost government documents are a real pain point for Kenyan citizens; so I built a system to fix that. That same mindset drives everything I build: find a problem, understand it well, then engineer a clean solution.

My technical range covers frontend development (React, HTML/CSS), backend systems (Node.js, Express), databases (MongoDB), and extends into Web3 territory I've worked with the Solana blockchain and ICP canisters. I'm comfortable on Ubuntu, deploy with Vercel and Render, and maintain a clean Git workflow.

I'm open to full-time roles, internships, and freelance collaborations particularly in Kenya's growing tech space and outside country too. If you need someone who can go from idea to deployed product, let's talk.
